# Finale proposal: the crown of the river ruins

## Scope and status

Requested on 2026-11-05: a final **120–150-second** first-success section after the second checkpoint. The first implementation was added on 2026-11-08; the design below remains the acceptance target, not a claim of human timing or final approval. The introduction, extended ascent, approved movement, camera scale and exactly two recovery checkpoints are preserved. The former provisional exit on the second checkpoint terrace moves to the actual end.

The preferred working target is **135 seconds**, including brief orientation but excluding failures, pauses and the optional route. With the scripted current route baseline of 183.16 seconds, the combined planning estimate is 303–333 seconds (5:03–5:33). This arithmetic is not a human measurement: the extended ascent still needs a new player timing. The earlier human result was 62.2 seconds intro and 92.6 seconds before the ascent extension.

The finale resolves Level 1 through confident traversal and a memorable destination. It introduces no enemy, ability, collectible requirement, mandatory detour, third checkpoint or boss. Completion UI, audio, persistent results and automatic menu transitions remain separate work under the existing exit contract.

## Playable sequence

Times are cumulative design budgets, not mandatory waits. The climax belongs to the final traversal; the last safe walk releases its pressure.

| 85–115 s | **Along the broken crown.** A short rising/falling chain crosses the open face of the valley. Do not enlarge the final walk, slow Mika, repeat a platform pattern or increase lift waiting to fill time. If the section is long or frustrating, shorten repeated combinations first.

## Geometry and physical rules

### Required obstacles and direction changes

User refinement on 2026-11-05: the main route must contain physical obstacles and occasional short mandatory reversals, rather than continuous forward jumping. Include two distinct readable switchbacks within the existing time budget:

1. **Courtyard wall:** an intact wall blocks the direct rightward route. A visible lower opening leads past its base; Mika then travels left for approximately 6–10 units along a sheltered terrace, climbs onto a broken stair and turns right above the wall. Reveal the stair and upper continuation together before the descent. The wall cannot be bypassed with an ordinary forward jump, but cannot trap Mika on either side. This is a compact bend in the required route, not a long backtrack through completed content.
2. **Crown buttress:** a substantial broken pier interrupts the upper path. Approach on broad ground, jump left onto a visible raised ledge behind the approach, then right onto the pier's broad crown and onward. Tune height and spacing so the intermediate landing is necessary with the actual motor, without a maximum-height or pixel-perfect jump. This reversal uses height rather than repeating the courtyard's low passage.

Between these beats, use a small number of grounded rubble blocks or fallen masonry pieces that Mika can jump over. Give each a clear silhouette and a generous landing beyond it. Obstacles must be physical foreground structures with source-matched collision, distinct from the softer noncolliding scenic ruin. No spikes, damage, crouch requirement, movable block puzzle or new interaction is introduced. Low passages must clear Mika's full standing/running collider with margin; jumping into a solid underside must stop normally without sticking or freezing animation.

At each reversal, frame the immediate landing in the same viewport as Mika before the jump. Ease horizontal camera look-ahead through the turn so it does not continue hiding the leftward route or snap when input changes. Do not rely on arrows, floating text, collectible trails or trial-and-error falls to explain the path. Grayscale and small-window review must distinguish the blocked forward route from the usable return ledge. Verify actual traversal cannot simply skip the switchback, while local recovery still returns to a meaningful attempt in under 15 seconds. Include explicit real-physics traversal, wall-contact idle and camera-direction-change coverage for both bends.
