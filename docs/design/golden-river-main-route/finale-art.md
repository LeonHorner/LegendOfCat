## Integration decisions

Platform contact rows and landing fractions are registered independently of grass tufts: wall 0.60/0.94, pier 0.675/0.92, garden 0.655/0.94. Each new form has its own solid collision underside; decorative ivy is excluded. Existing rooted crossings keep their original family. Large terminal ground keeps the native-aspect tall cliff artwork, whose feet extend below water. Fallen masonry rests on broad roof terraces; its elevation was reduced after the first render exposed a slight gap.

The new hillside uses one absolute anchor and **0.22 camera travel**, without recycling or a late clamp. The original 0.08 factor was rejected as appearing to stop. Its hill continues below the falling-camera envelope. The gate is world-space, has no checkpoint behavior and is distinct from the checkpoint arch. Its feet are embedded and covered by the exact registered terminal-bank foliage. Both counterweight frames use corresponding catch-terrace foliage in front of their feet. Architecture behind the gameplay plane has no collision; the visible moving decks and solid obstacles do.

The user rejected using a grass-topped floating pier sprite as a ground obstacle. The replacement rubble has a flat lower base, a stepped solid collision contour and a narrower central top. Contact UV is 0.665 and the resting-base UV is 0.17. Its 0.82-unit elevation embeds the base approximately 0.005 units into the terrace; exact source-registered terrace grass overlaps its foot. The corresponding tests distinguish a grounded obstacle's solid lower corners from the empty lower corners of floating shelves, and verify safe recovery onto actual stone steps rather than assuming every obstacle has a full-width flat cap.
