# Finale art and review record

## Sources and creative direction — 2026-11-08

All new paintings are original generated project assets made with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool; the exact underlying model identifier was not exposed. No third-party reference, licensed character, font or stock image was used. Project-owned generated work, with no additional third-party redistribution restriction. The existing selected masonry artwork was inspected as a style reference. Human direction preserves Golden River's painted gold foliage, blue-violet stone and teal ivy, requires actual new platform anatomy, and rejects pale photorealistic stone, clipped ends and copied sky.

The final imported files are in `Assets/Game/Resources/GoldenRiver`. Unity creates their metadata and imports them uncompressed, with no mipmaps, Clamp wrapping and preserved transparency. Full source compositions for the extended landscapes are in the adjacent `sources` folder; the prior introduction/ascent compositions remain available there.

## Integration decisions

Platform contact rows and landing fractions are registered independently of grass tufts: wall 0.60/0.94, pier 0.675/0.92, garden 0.655/0.94. Each new form has its own solid collision underside; decorative ivy is excluded. Existing rooted crossings keep their original family. Large terminal ground keeps the native-aspect tall cliff artwork, whose feet extend below water. Fallen masonry rests on broad roof terraces; its elevation was reduced after the first render exposed a slight gap.

The new hillside uses one absolute anchor and **0.22 camera travel**, without recycling or a late clamp. The original 0.08 factor was rejected as appearing to stop. Its hill continues below the falling-camera envelope. The gate is world-space, has no checkpoint behavior and is distinct from the checkpoint arch. Its feet are embedded and covered by the exact registered terminal-bank foliage. Both counterweight frames use corresponding catch-terrace foliage in front of their feet. Architecture behind the gameplay plane has no collision; the visible moving decks and solid obstacles do.

The user rejected using a grass-topped floating pier sprite as a ground obstacle. The replacement rubble has a flat lower base, a stepped solid collision contour and a narrower central top. Contact UV is 0.665 and the resting-base UV is 0.17. Its 0.82-unit elevation embeds the base approximately 0.005 units into the terrace; exact source-registered terrace grass overlaps its foot. The corresponding tests distinguish a grounded obstacle's solid lower corners from the empty lower corners of floating shelves, and verify safe recovery onto actual stone steps rather than assuming every obstacle has a full-width flat cap. Pier artwork remains on suspended steps only.

PhotoCraft's headless engine extended the near panorama from 8688×1448 to 12784×1448 and the far from 4344×724 to 6392×724. The new strips were resampled at matching height and joined with 248/124 source-pixel overlap; retained content was registered vertically after copying. A narrow source-only mask overlap blends the landscape seam, without fading building bases or painting a blur over the skyline. Native layered files are `sources/finale-near-panorama.pcraft` and `sources/finale-far-panorama.pcraft`. Runtime retains the old intro/ascent pixel scale and keep placement while appending the new coverage. Selected travel remains 0.14/0.08 and does not freeze at the end. No new landscape tiling or mirrored ruin is introduced.

The first motion review exposed vertical skyline steps at both extension joins. The final edit replaces the near strip's large left tree/high mountain with a low wooded ridge and lowers the far strip's left hill. PhotoCraft then trims only the terminal joining ridge silhouettes with irregular, unfeathered contour masks; the separate narrow rasterized overlap controls color registration below that edge. This removes the vertical walls without blurring the skyline or fading terrain into empty space. The full introduction and its existing landmarks remain outside these terminal join regions. The native documents retain both source layers and their masks. The inclined guide is lowered so its beam/rollers meet the stone underside rather than crossing Mika's body; its feet are embedded behind the catch terrace's registered foreground.

## Validation boundary

Local captures and the scripted physics recording are implementation review evidence, not human timing or real player-input recordings. Review at 960×540, 640×360 and grayscale includes roof obstacles, both switchbacks, both movers and the final gate. Final test/build counts and remaining acceptance are recorded in `finale.md`. No new Easter Egg, completion screen, persistent-data format or audio is part of this work.
