# Shark breach artwork

The realistic great white shark atlas was generated on 2026-11-13 with OpenAI
ImageGen. The exact model ID was unavailable. PhotoCraft 0.23.1 MCP cropped the
transparent atlas to 2172 × 330 pixels and exported the three registered jaw
poses to `Assets/Game/Resources/WaterRecovery/shark-breach-frames.png`.

The requested direction was a more realistic shark that breaches high and catches
Mika from above. The animation follows an arc and pitches the shark downward
before the jaws close. Separate takeoff and landing splashes mark both contacts.
The artwork is original generated material under the repository asset terms.
